The golden rules
  1. Type the code yourself. Copy-pasting skips the part where your brain learns.
  2. Break things on purpose. Remove a ?, rename a variable, read the compiler error.
  3. Do the exercises before the solutions. Struggling for 10 minutes is the lesson.
  4. Build something of your own after stage 3 — even a tiny command-line tool.
  5. Mark lessons complete to track progress (saved in your browser).
